# Randos Philosophy

Randos is an anonymous, audio-only language conversation app built around low pressure human interaction.

It is not a social network.

It is not a content platform.

It is not a metrics-driven language app.

Randos is closer to public conversational infrastructure: a calm, minimal place where adults can briefly meet through language and then disappear.

## Core Experience

The user chooses:

- the language they will speak
- the language they want to hear

Then Randos pairs them with a compatible stranger for a short audio conversation.

Example:

- User A speaks English and wants to hear Spanish
- User B speaks Spanish and wants to hear English

This naturally supports:

- crosstalk
- language exchange
- listening practice
- casual conversation

without forcing users into rigid modes or labels.

## Product Constraints

Randos is intentionally constrained:

- no accounts
- no profiles
- no contacts
- no followers
- no feeds
- no ads
- no streaks
- no gamification
- no permanent social graph
- no video
- no recording
- no server-side audio access
- no eavesdropping
- no admin listening

Audio-only is a permanent product constraint.

## Privacy and Ephemerality

Conversations are private and ephemeral by default.

The server coordinates conversations, but it does not process, store, inspect, or record audio.

Users can leave instantly at any time for any reason.

A conversation only continues if both people mutually agree to extend it.

## Time Limits

Random calls start with a five minute limit.

At the end of each five minute segment, both users may choose whether to continue.

The call only extends if both users agree.

The maximum total call duration is thirty minutes.

After that, users must return to the queue.

This keeps Randos focused on brief, low pressure encounters rather than becoming a private calling app.

## Adult Use

Randos is intended for adults 18 and older.

Before entering the queue, users must acknowledge:

> I am 18 or older and understand that conversations are anonymous and unmoderated.

This is intentionally lightweight.

Randos does not use invasive age verification, accounts, identity checks, or surveillance mechanisms.

## Design Values

Randos prioritizes:

- spontaneity
- emotional safety
- simplicity
- human presence
- lightweight practice without commitment

The interface should feel calm, minimal, mobile friendly, and non-performative.

Avoid dashboards, scores, ratings, avatars, profiles, online counts, streaks, and other social media clutter.

The product should feel like a doorway, not a platform.
